# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Treat people with respect and evaluate contributions on their technical merits.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive feedback with specific evidence and proposed improvements.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Protect other people's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, sexualized behavior, and disclosure of private information without consent are not acceptable. This applies to repository issues, pull requests, and project-related community interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at jahwanthpulugujju@gmail.com. Include links or context when available; avoid posting sensitive reports in public issues. Reports will be handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or block participants according to the severity and recurrence of the behavior.

This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors.
